  • Scale. Every engine reports a Z-score on the same −3 to +3 scale. Zero is historical equilibrium.
  • Long-term expectations vs short-term signals. Long-term rows describe what markets are paying investors to hold risk over years. Short-term rows are signals that can change quickly and are poor predictors of near-term direction on their own.
  • Units. Following Team 1's conventions, rates and returns are stored as decimals (0.025 means 2.5%) and shown here as percent. 3M and 1Y columns show the change in the level over that period: percentage points (pp) for decimal series, index points otherwise.
  • Data status. Every row carries Team 1's data_status: real, synthetic or placeholder. Only real rows count as reporting; synthetic and placeholder rows are labelled and should not be read as market signals.
  • Validation. "Checked" means Team 4 has validated the result. All other rows are shown as not yet validated.
  • Sources. Each row lists its planned source. The data date and release_id come from the loaded CSV files.
  • Volatility row. Level 14.28, standard Z-score −0.66 and downside Z-score −1.63 come from the strategy memo of Sep 22, 2026 (10-year mean about 19.50; standard deviation about 7.90; downside deviation about 3.20).
