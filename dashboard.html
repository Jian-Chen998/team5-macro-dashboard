  • Long-term expectations vs short-term signals. Long-term rows describe what markets are paying investors to hold risk over years. Short-term rows are signals that can change quickly and are poor predictors of near-term direction on their own.
  • Changes. 3M and 1Y columns show the change in the level over that period, in the unit shown.
  • Validation. "Checked" means Team 4 has validated the result. All other rows are shown as not yet validated.
